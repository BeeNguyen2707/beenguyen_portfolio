The Peregrine

The Peregrine is a concept aircraft designed by me and my 5 other Aerospace Engineering colleagues.

This aircraft is a business turboprop designed for the European market, with a maximum range of 1400 km and a top speed of 560 km/h.

The Peregrine has a wingspan of 17m and a total length of 15.3m.

An MTOW of 4.9 tons allows the Peregrine to carry 9 passengers.

The twin turboprop engines can deliver 1700 shaft horsepower.
