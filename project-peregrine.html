The Peregrine

The Peregrine is a high-performance concept aircraft designed as a collaborative effort by a team of six Aerospace Engineering colleagues.

Tailored for the European business market, this turboprop features a 1400 km range and a top speed of 560 km/h.

With a 17m wingspan and an MTOW of 4.9 tons, the aircraft is engineered to comfortably carry 9 passengers.

Power is delivered via twin turboprop engines producing 1700 shaft horsepower.
