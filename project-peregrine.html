The Peregrine

The Peregrine is a concept aircraft designed by me and five Aerospace Engineering colleagues.

A business turboprop tailored for the European market, featuring a 1400 km range and a maximum velocity of 560 km/h.

With a 17m wingspan and 15.3m length, the aircraft supports a 4.9 ton MTOW and accommodates 9 passengers.

The propulsion system utilizes twin turboprop engines delivering 1700 shaft horsepower.
