Concept Rescue Drone

This is a small project for my Mechanical Drawing class in Fall 2024.

My task was to 3D design an octo-rotor drone that is capable of carrying 2 different cameras, as well as 4 different sensors.

The purpose of the drone is to do rescue and surveillance missions. In more depth, the drone is installed with 2 cameras: one provides real images while the other provides thermal images.

There are 4 different sensors available: 2 anti-collision sensors, 1 altimeter, 1 GPS, and 1 humidity sensor, which could be used to predict wildfire.

The design allows the main body to have more space on the bottom since the drone doesn't require a landing gear, as well as with the huge amount of lift generated by 8 brushless motors, the drone could be equipped with larger cameras if needed.
