Thermal Electric Vehicle

This project explores using a thermal electric generator (TEG) to power a custom electric vehicle.

The chassis is constructed from bamboo and rigid cardboard, utilizing a tricycle configuration to minimize weight. The assembly features modular generator and motor units, powered by a 3-5V geared motor.

The core power unit is a TEG sandwiched between a lower heating chamber and an upper ice reservoir.

A key mechanical feature is the swing-mount system, which ensures the generator remains vertical even on inclines, optimizing the heat transfer efficiency.

Heat is provided by a modified tea light candle. To optimize distribution across the TEG surface, a 0.3mm aluminum interface plate was bonded using high-conductivity thermal adhesive tape.

Testing yielded a peak output of 2.4V and a velocity of 0.08 m/s.

While successful on flat terrain, future iterations will include a secondary gear reduction stage to improve climbing torque for steeper gradients.
