Thermal Electric Vehicle

This project explores utilizing a thermal electric generator (TEG) to power a custom-built electric vehicle.

The chassis is constructed from bamboo and rigid cardboard, featuring a tricycle-wheel configuration to minimize weight while maintaining structural integrity.

A key innovation is the swing-mount system, which ensures the generator remains perfectly vertical even when navigating ramps.

This design optimizes the thermal gradient between the hot and cold junctions, significantly improving heat transfer efficiency.

The heat source is a modified tea light candle placed in a lower chamber.

To maximize coverage, a 0.3mm aluminum interface plate was bonded to the TEG using high-conductivity thermal adhesive tape.

Empirical testing reached a peak output of 2.4V with a sustained velocity of 0.08 m/s.

Future iterations will implement an secondary gear reduction stage to overcome torque limitations on 20-degree gradients.
