Thermal Electric Vehicle

This project is to use a thermal electric generator (TEG) to power an electric car.

The body of the car is made of bamboo sticks and hard cardboard, with a tricycle-wheel configuration to reduce weight. The vehicle has two detachable parts: the generator, and the motor, which is a 3-5V, 200 RPM motor, with a reduction gearbox.

The generator is basically a TEG with a heating chamber on the bottom, and an ice container on top of it. Moreover, the generator is placed on a swing system, therefore, even when the vehicle goes on the ramp, the generator will always be vertical. In theory, this design further optimizes the heat transfer between the hot side and the cold side of the TEG.

The heat source for this vehicle is a tea light with a modified wick to make the fire larger.

The tea light is placed below the TEG, in the heating chamber. To ensure that the heat is distributed on the whole surface of the TEG, a 0.3mm piece of aluminum is installed on the bottom plate with thermal adhesive tape.

After testing, the maximum voltage generated was 2.4V, and the average speed of the car is 0.08 m/s.

However, the torque from the motor is not enough for the car to climb a 20 degrees ramp. This can be solved by adding an addition gear reduction as the gearbox that comes with the motor doesn't generate enough torque.
