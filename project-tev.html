Thermal Electric Vehicle

This project explores using a thermal electric generator (TEG) to power a custom electric vehicle.

The chassis is constructed from bamboo and rigid cardboard, utilizing a tricycle configuration to minimize mass. The assembly features modular generator and motor units, powered by a 3-5V geared motor.

The core power unit is a TEG sandwiched between a lower heating chamber and an upper ice reservoir.

A key mechanical feature is the swing-mount system, which ensures the generator remains perfectly vertical even while navigating inclines, maximizing the thermal gradient efficiency between the hot and cold junctions.

Heat is provided by a modified tea light candle with an enhanced wick for higher thermal output.

To optimize heat distribution across the TEG surface, a 0.3mm aluminum interface plate was bonded to the assembly using high-conductivity thermal adhesive tape.

Empirical testing yielded a peak output of 2.4V, achieving a sustained velocity of 0.08 m/s.

While successful on flat terrain, current motor torque limitations prevent the vehicle from conquering 20-degree gradients—a challenge that could be addressed through an secondary reduction stage in the drive transmission.
